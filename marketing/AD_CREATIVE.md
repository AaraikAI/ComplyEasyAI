## ✅ Creative Testing Framework

### A/B Testing Priority

**High Priority Tests:**
1. Headline: ROI-focused vs Pain-focused vs Feature-focused
2. CTA: "Book Demo" vs "Start Free Trial" vs "Download Guide"
3. Visual: Product screenshot vs Diagram vs Person/team
4. Length: Short copy vs Long copy
5. Proof: Stats vs Testimonials vs Logos

**Testing Schedule:**
- Week 1-2: Headline variations
- Week 3-4: CTA variations
- Week 5-6: Visual variations
- Week 7-8: Copy length variations
- Ongoing: Proof element variations
