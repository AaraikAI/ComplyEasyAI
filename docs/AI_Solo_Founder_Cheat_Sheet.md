- 7:15-7:30am: Respond to hot leads via email (15 min)
- 7:30-9:30am: Conduct demos (4 × 30 min)

**Midday (1 hour):**

- 12:30-1:00pm: Support escalations (if any)

**Afternoon (2 hours):**
- 3:00-5:00pm: Product development / deep work

**What AI Does While You Sleep:**
- Finds leads (Clay)

- Analyze what worked/what didn't
- Plan next week's campaigns

**Sunday Morning (2 hours):**
- Generate 30 days of content with Claude
- Schedule everything in Buffer
- You're done for the month

**Total:** 30 hours/week (vs 80 hours doing manually)

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: DON'T SKIP

1. **Email Warmup:** 2 weeks minimum before sending campaigns
2. **Content Batching:** Create 30 days at once, not daily
3. **Qualification:** Only demo qualified leads (BANT)
4. **Product Quality:** Fix bugs faster than adding features
5. **Metrics:** Check daily, optimize weekly, pivot monthly

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

1. Spamming cold emails day 1 (instant blacklist)
2. Perfect product paralysis (ship & iterate)
3. Ignoring customer feedback (build what they want)
4. Scaling broken campaigns (fix, then scale)
5. Hiring too early (AI can handle 90% until $2M ARR)
